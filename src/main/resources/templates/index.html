A LITTLE PREPARATION GOES A LONG WAY

Get connected before
you need a helping hand.

A few simple steps bring your neighbourhood, your family, and local responders into one place.

01 / MAKE YOURSELF KNOWN

Join your quarter.

Create your account, choose your quarter, and add your identification pictures.

02 / BRING YOUR PEOPLE CLOSER

Connect your family.

Create a household group or enter an invite code when registering to join an existing one.

03 / REACH OUT WHEN IT MATTERS

Send an emergency alert.

From your dashboard, share your current location with local authorities and your family group.
