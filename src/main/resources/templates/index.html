Before you get started

What do I need to register?

Your name, email address, a password of at least 7 characters, your quarter, and two identification pictures. Authorities register with their service and badge details.

How do location and notifications work?

Allow location access when sending an alert. Enable sound from your dashboard and keep the page open to hear incoming notifications.

How do I add my family?

Create a family group after signing in, then share its invite code. New household members enter that code when registering.
